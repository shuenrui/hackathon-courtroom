# Hackathon Theme — #BuildForMsia

**Build AI Solutions for Better Public Services in Malaysia**

Use AI and Malaysian government data to solve a real problem affecting the public.

Build something that makes public services safer, simpler or more accessible. Focus on
one specific user, one clear problem and one practical outcome that can be demonstrated
during the hackathon.

Possible areas include service accessibility, public information, safety and resilience,
service delivery, and community participation.

## Problem Statement

Describe the problem in one sentence:

> *[Target user] struggles to [do something] because [specific barrier], resulting in
> [real impact].*

## Guidelines

Your solution should:
- Meaningfully use at least one dataset from data.gov.my
- Address a real need faced by Malaysians or public-service teams
- Demonstrate a practical, working outcome
- Be useful, inclusive and realistic
- Clearly communicate data sources, limitations and uncertainty

## Suggested Tech Stack

Teams are encouraged to explore:
- **Qwen** for understanding, reasoning and multilingual interactions
- **OpenClaw** for managing agents, workflows, tools and user interactions
- **Devin** to help build, test and ship quickly

> **One user. One public problem. One working outcome.**

---

## How the theme binds judging (internal)

- **problem_fit** is assessed against this theme: a real Malaysian public-service need,
  a specific target user, a clear barrier, and real impact. Off-theme or generic problems
  score low on problem_fit even when well written.
- **completeness** inherits "one practical outcome demonstrable during the hackathon" —
  the URL smoke test remains the authority on whether the outcome works.
- **solution_quality** weighs meaningful use of data.gov.my / Malaysian government data
  and honest communication of data sources, limitations and uncertainty.
- The suggested tech stack is **encouragement, not a requirement** — never penalize a
  team for not using Qwen/OpenClaw/Devin, and never reward stack claims the evidence
  does not support.
